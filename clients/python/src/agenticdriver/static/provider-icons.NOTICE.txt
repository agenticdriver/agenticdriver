This collection began with the 262 SVGs maintained in hashimkarim/usagestat-bar.
provenance.json records that source revision and the original file hashes.
Its history credits LobeHub lobe-icons and steipete/CodexBar alongside local
UsageStat-Bar additions. Their MIT notices are retained in licenses/.

Names and logos are trademarks of their respective owners. The library does
not imply endorsement or transfer trademark rights. Product alternatives and
aliases select artwork only; they do not select a runtime, account or billing.

The generated inline module removes presentation styles, accessibility labels
and titles and namespaces SVG fragment identifiers. Original SVG files remain
unchanged. Consumers supply their own accessible label or mark icons decorative.
