Develop three distinct brand directions for AgenticDriver (agenticdriver.dev).
It is an SDK for TypeScript, Python, Go and Rust applications. Developers connect
their own provider API accounts or supported native subscription sessions.
Execution can happen locally or through a secure connection to a remote host.
Applications explicitly select a provider, account connection and model.
The audience is developers building research assistants, email workspaces and
creative tools. Keep the name AgenticDriver. Do not promise universal provider
compatibility, unlimited usage, automatic model routing or guaranteed accuracy.
For each direction, give a tagline, two hex colours, a visual motif and one
sentence explaining why it fits the audience. Stay below 220 words in total.
